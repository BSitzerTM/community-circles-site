**Participatory budgeting experiment (exploration).** Based on a public participation practice (https://vimeo.com/162743651). The proposal is to start generating economic impact through the CCME beyond big events. Idea: a monthly membership fee of about R50, which everyone in the meeting is comfortable with. It welcomes anyone who wants to give more, and discounts anyone who wants to pay less. Budgeting stays transparent from day one in the community WhatsApp group, showing where money is spent and inviting ideas on what to spend it on. Decisions are made sociocratically.
