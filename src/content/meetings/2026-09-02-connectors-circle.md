**Street updates (Mike and Charles).** Goal: unify existing WhatsApp street groups under the CCME platform to improve connection and information flow. The Axeman street group was integrated successfully, which validated the model. Mike contacted group admins individually. The integration method must be flexible. For streets with little group activity, a new CCME-hosted group may work better than migrating an old one. Charles has admin approval to activate the Dartmouth group's integration. Key features: new members need admin approval, street admins keep full control of their group, and street groups receive only a quarterly CCME report.

**Council ward committees (Charles).** Ward committees are compulsory, legislated bodies that will reform after the upcoming elections, likely in December or January. They give a formal channel to the ward councillor, which can be critical for escalating issues, and offer insight into city-level plans. CCME will stay non-partisan and work with any elected councillor. The team believes CCME's community-led structure is more effective than the committee's top-down model. The team will monitor the committee to avoid mission drift and resource drain.

**Admin-only group for certain posts (Brad).** Problem: announcements from initiatives create clutter in member-facing WhatsApp groups. Solution: a new admin-only WhatsApp group for central announcements, where admins can post but chat is turned off. Initiative leads send announcements to a designated poster, initially Brad. Decision: approved for a trial period, then refined based on feedback.

**Branding ideas.** Brad presented an AI-generated branding package (logo, colours, fonts). Decision: rejected, because it conflicts with CCME's human-centred, community-driven ethos. Corli will lead a new community-driven design project. Timeline: a simple, usable logo is needed by the end of the month. Goal: a minimalist logo that is easy to apply across all media. Corli will recruit community members (for example Alfreda and Lauren). Brad will provide the AI prompts as a starting point, including the insight that "Connected Community, Merini State" suggests the name could extend beyond the current area.
