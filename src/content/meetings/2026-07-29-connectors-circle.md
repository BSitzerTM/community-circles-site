**Policy proposal: Participation Steward (WhatsApp) role.** Each community group will appoint one or more Conversation Stewards. This role balances freedom of expression with the shared purpose of each group. A Conversation Steward is not a moderator or enforcer. They gently guide discussions back to the group's purpose when they drift well off topic, encourage appropriate sharing, and help members understand the group's intent. They also look for creative ways to include quieter members, using polls, questions and prompts. The role is separate from the Group Leader role. The community will decide the election and implementation process separately, and the policy may be reviewed periodically.
