**Purpose.** The General Community Circle is now the central hub for decision-making, connecting all community groups and external entities. Core purpose: "to be a more connected community".

**Internal groups:** Repair Cafe, Learning Circle, Project Play, Film Circle, Recycling & Waste Collective, Tech for Good, and others.

**External entities:** MLRA, MID, the Ward Councillor, Zandvlei Trust, local businesses, Back2Work Campaign, and others.

**Leadership roles:** Leader Brad Sitzer. Facilitator Charles Croeser. Administrator/Secretary: vacant.

**Decision: MLRA Advisory Circle.** The Muizenberg Lakeside Residents Association (MLRA) is being resurrected after being dormant since about 2020, with an AGM on 22 July. Proposal: create an MLRA Advisory Circle within the General Community Circle as a formal liaison, so the GCC is informed and can give input on the MLRA's direction. Approved by consent. Leadership: Corli (Lead) and Charles Croeser (Delegate).

**Decision: closing dormant groups.** "Activating Common Spaces" and "Learning Circle" have been inactive since October 2025. Proposal: formally close both, to declutter the WhatsApp community and focus energy on active projects. Approved by consent.

**Note.** The Tech for Good group, also dormant, will be reactivated by Brad Sitzer, who is taking personal accountability for it.

**Compost bins at Muizenberg High School (Ruby).** Use three unused compost bin spaces at the school. Proposal: work with the school's art teacher to build composting and upcycling into the curriculum, possibly ending in an exhibition at Rhodes Cottage. No formal decision was made. The idea was supported, and Charles Croeser offered to help.

**Follow-up.** Create the MLRA Advisory Circle WhatsApp group and invite members. Discuss the ward committee with Cllr Mandy Marr at the next meeting. Close the two dormant WhatsApp groups. Brad to send the CCME purpose, values and group agreements PDF to attendees.
