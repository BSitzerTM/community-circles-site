**Entrepreneurship Development Program (Wendy).** A "collective conscious learning set" method, developed by Wendy during her PhD, to grow entrepreneurs. Small, diverse groups of four to five people meet weekly. A facilitator removes ego, power and control, which builds empathy and non-judgement. Entrepreneurs bring a challenge, and the group only asks questions to find new solutions. Goal: grow entrepreneurs by 15 to 30 percent a year. Launch plan: phase 1 is an "expression of interest" flyer to gauge demand. Phase 2 is the full launch, once there is enough interest. Needs: a free venue for weekly sessions for 15 to 20 people, and group help distributing flyers. Wendy will write a public summary of the methodology, protecting her intellectual property.
