Air quality

There is no air quality monitoring station in Ward 45 or anywhere in Subcouncil 19. The City's network has 12 stations, all north of the False Bay coast. The nearest to Ward 45 is . Readings from these stations describe the city's air generally; they are not measurements of Muizenberg's own air.

Station positions are approximate: the City's data does not include coordinates, so each station is placed at the centre of its suburb. Rings show 10, 20 and 30 km from the centre of Ward 45.

Yearly averages

Only years with at least about a quarter of hours measured are shown. "Coverage" is the share of the year with a valid reading. Exceedances count hours or days above the national standard listed above the chart. The data file is titled 2022 to 2024 but includes earlier years for some stations.
