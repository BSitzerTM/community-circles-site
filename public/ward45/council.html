Subcouncil 19: budget, planning & arrears

Ward 45 sits almost entirely within Subcouncil 19, which stretches from Muizenberg and Lakeside south through Fish Hoek, Simon's Town, Ocean View, Kommetjie and Noordhoek. This page covers the subcouncil's operating budget allocation, the planning designations on the map, and municipal arrears.

What's in the portal and what isn't. The open data has no subcouncil meeting minutes, agendas or plans. Those sit in the City's council calendar and document centre. The budget figures below come from the City's published budget annexures, not the portal.
