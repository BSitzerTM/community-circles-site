Every City service request (complaint or fault) logged since January 2023 for locations inside Ward 45, from the City's spatial service requests layer. Filter by complaint type and year, and see how long each type takes to close.

Scope and limits. Requests are placed on the map by their recorded coordinates. The City's suburb label is shown where recorded, and about 1,400 requests in the ward have no suburb label. Completion times are the gap between the created and completed dates, so requests still open are left out of those figures.
