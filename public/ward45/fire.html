Fire and rescue call-outs for Ward 45's suburbs and the wider Muizenberg area, from the City's incident records for 2009 to 2022. The records carry a suburb and street but no coordinates, so the map shades suburbs by incident count. Four fire stations serve Subcouncil 19; none sits inside Ward 45.

Read the trend with care. Recorded incidents drop sharply after 2018 (see the chart). That looks like incomplete recording in the later file, not a real fall in incidents, so don't treat 2019 to 2022 as a decline.
