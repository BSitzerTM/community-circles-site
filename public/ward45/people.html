No population history in the portal. The City publishes one population estimate (2025) per suburb. For change over time, this page uses the valuation rolls (2012, 2015, 2018, 2022, 2025), which record median home values and the number of residential properties. Ward 45's boundary doesn't follow suburb lines, so suburb figures can't be summed into a ward total.
