Ward 45 · Marina Estate

A data report on Ward 45 (Muizenberg, Lakeside and Marina da Gama) and its subcouncil, built from the City of Cape Town's open data portal. Ward 45 is part of Subcouncil 19, which runs south along the False Bay coast to Noordhoek. Each page has an interactive map: switch between Ward 45 and the whole subcouncil, click anything for detail.

What isn't in the open data

  • Crime. No crime dataset in the portal; the StreetSignal storymap is the closest source.
  • Outdoor gyms. Listed on the portal without coordinates or a download, so they can't be mapped.
  • Ward-level budgets. Budget files have no ward field. The subcouncil allocation annexures give amounts for Subcouncil 19 only.
  • Subcouncil minutes, agendas and plans. Held in the City's council calendar and document centre, not the portal.
  • Population history. Only a 2025 estimate per suburb; the valuation rolls (2012 to 2025) show change in homes and values.
  • Below-ground electricity plans. Not published. Streetlights (above-ground public lighting) and distribution capacity are in the portal.
  • Air quality at Muizenberg. No monitoring station in the subcouncil; the nearest are 15 to 20 km away.
  • Homeless shelters. The City's list has none in Muizenberg; the nearest are at Kalk Bay and Simon's Town.
