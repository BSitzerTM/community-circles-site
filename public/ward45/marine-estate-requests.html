Marine Estate service requests

City service requests logged inside the Marine Estate, Muizenberg, since January 2023. Shows where requests cluster, which faults recur, and how long completed requests took. Data runs to 11 November 2026.

Read this first. The City's data has no status field, so a missing completion date does not mean a call is still open; spot checks found most of them were closed on the City's tracking site. The estate boundary is hand-drawn and approximate, and requests within 40 m of it are included because the City places some requests on the nearest street or erf. Medians are based on the dates logged, not on site work or service standards.

Most-repeated faults in the estate

Groups of three or more requests of the same type within about 35 m of each other, all time. Circle sizes on the map follow these counts.

How long completed requests took

Median days from the logged date to the completed date, for complaint types with at least five completed requests. Only requests that have a completion date are counted.
